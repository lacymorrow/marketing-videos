## How to record so the fear does not matter

- Screen first, silent. Follow the beat sheet for timing. Nobody is watching and every beat except the ShipX publish can be re-run.
- Voice after, over the footage, reading `narration.txt`. Two or three takes. Pick the one that sounds like talking to a friend. Nobody sees a face. Hands and voice only, by design.
- Rough cut is the goal. QuickTime for the screen, iMovie or Descript for the voice pass. No zooms, no music, no end card. Post it, then improve the next one.
- Read each `narration.txt` aloud twice before the voice pass. Rewrite any line you trip on, in your own words. The file is a teleprompter, not a contract.
