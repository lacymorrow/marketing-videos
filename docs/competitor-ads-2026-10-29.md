## Where Juno is different (use, do not hide)
- Runs on YOUR Mac, inside your real apps and logins. Theirs: a cloud computer (dots, Grok Bot) or a phone app (Muse). Nothing leaves the machine except the model call.
- Voice first: hold a key, talk, watch it happen. Siri voice replies.
- Open source (FSL), one person, no data dossier (TIME, 2026-10-06, on Muse).
- Honest gaps: Juno does not work with the laptop closed, has no cloud computer, no iMessage/WhatsApp inbound yet. Do not claim these.
