Banana Bread

Makes one loaf. 1 hour 10 minutes.

3 ripe bananas, mashed
1/3 cup melted butter
3/4 cup brown sugar
1 egg, beaten
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1 teaspoon baking soda
A pinch of salt
1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1/2 cup chopped walnuts

Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a loaf pan. Mix the butter into the bananas, then stir in the sugar, egg and vanilla. Sprinkle in the baking soda and salt, then fold in the flour and walnuts. Pour into the pan and bake for about an hour, until a toothpick comes out clean.
