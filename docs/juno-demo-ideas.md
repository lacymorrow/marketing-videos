# Juno demo ideas (2026-10-30)

Rule: every demo is a job the model cannot do by answering. One job per ad. Spoken line first, then what is on screen, then why a chatbot loses. Staged data only; no real names, contacts or accounts.

## Work

| Spoken line | On screen | Why Juno wins |
|---|---|---|
| "Hi Juno, put every invoice PDF from this month into a folder called October invoices and tell me the total." | Finder gathers the PDFs, Preview flashes open, Siri voice says the total | It has the files. ChatGPT needs you to upload them one by one. |
| "Turn the action items in the meeting notes on my Desktop into Reminders, due Friday." | Notes file read, Reminders fills in | Reads a local file and writes into an Apple app nobody has an API for. |
| "Rename each PDF in this folder to what it actually is." | Three generic filenames become real ones, one by one | Reads content, acts on filenames. Pure computer use. |
| "Fill out this form with the details from the PDF on my Desktop." | Browser form fills itself from a local document | Two apps, one request. Cloud agents never see your Desktop. |
| "Export this sheet as a PDF and start an email to the client with it attached." | Numbers export, Mail compose opens, attachment lands, Juno stops at Send | The approval pause is the feature. Show it waiting for the click. |
| "Set me up for the presentation: Do Not Disturb on, close Slack and Messages, open the keynote on my Desktop." | Four things happen in order | Chained system control. Nobody types this in a chatbot. |
| "Read me the last three messages from the team chat, out loud." | Nothing opens, Siri voice reads | Eyes-free. The Mac answers while you make coffee. |
| "Keep an eye on this download and tell me when it finishes." | Juno watches the progress bar, speaks when done | Patience is a thing an agent on your machine can do. |
| "Compare the prices in these two tabs and tell me which is cheaper with shipping." | Two tabs, one spoken answer, the winner's tab comes to front | Reads your real browser session, including logged-in prices. |
| Dictation: hold the key and talk a messy paragraph into any text box. | Clean text appears with grammar fixed | Wispr Flow does this for $12 a month. Juno does it free. |

## Play

| Spoken line | On screen | Why Juno wins |
|---|---|---|
| "Find something groovy to listen to." (already a rig scene) | Music opens, a track plays | Voice to playback, no menu diving. |
| "Open Chess. I'll play white. Knight to f3." | Chess app, pieces move as you call them | Pure fun, and it proves click precision on a dense board. |
| "Set my wallpaper to the best photo from last weekend." | Photos browsed, Settings changed, desktop changes | Taste plus system control in one line. |
| "Find the cheapest flight to Denver next Friday and leave the tab open." | Browser search, result tab left open | Does the boring part, leaves you the decision. |
| "Make a playlist from the songs in this folder." | Finder to Music, playlist built | Files to app, a job with zero shortcuts today. |
| "Turn the recipe on this page into a shopping list in Reminders." | Web page read, Reminders list appears | Reads the screen you are looking at. |

## Computer use and housekeeping

| Spoken line | On screen | Why Juno wins |
|---|---|---|
| "Turn on Dark Mode, make the text bigger, and kill the notification sounds." | System Settings navigated three times | The thing everyone hates finding. Strongest mass-market demo. |
| "Clean my Desktop: screenshots, PDFs, installers in their own folders, trash the dmgs." | Desktop goes from chaos to four folders | The before and after sells itself. |
| "What is eating my disk? Show me the biggest files in Downloads." | Finder sorted by size, Juno says the top three | Answer plus the window to act on it. |
| "Uninstall that screen recorder, including the leftover files." | App trashed, Library folders cleaned | A real chore with a real risk, done where you can watch. |
| "Switch the sound to the TV and turn it up a bit." | Output device changes, volume moves | Quick, physical, satisfying. |
| "I can't see the screen right now. Read me what is in front of me." | Juno describes the window out loud | The accessibility story. Honest and strong. |
| "Translate the text in this window to Spanish and type it back in." | Selected text replaced | Reads the screen, writes to the screen. |
| "Every Friday at 4, zip this week's screenshots into Archive." | Scheduled job created, then the zip appears | The always-on line, earned. |
| "Stop." mid-task, then "Undo that." | Juno halts on the word, steps back one rung | Control is the trust demo. Pairs with "where you can watch it". |

## Film next (five, mapped to the ads that need them)

1. Dark Mode / text size / notification sounds. Slogan: "Juno gets things done." Everyone has felt this pain.
2. Clean my Desktop. Slogan: "Hand your work to Juno." Best before-and-after frame.
3. Fill the form from the PDF. Slogan: "The teammate that finishes the work." Two apps, one sentence.
4. Every Friday at 4, zip the screenshots. Slogan: "Always on. Built to handle your work." Unblocks the planned always-on ad.
5. Reply to the staged contact, then "Stop." on a second request. Slogan: "Works on your computer. Not someone else's." Shows control.

Each needs a staged folder or account on graphite before the rig runs it. Receipts and rename scenes already in scenes.txt stay ahead of these in the queue.
